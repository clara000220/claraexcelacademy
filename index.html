FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling.

Are these courses HRDC / HRD Corp claimable?▼
Yes — all courses are claimable under HRD Corp for eligible Malaysian employers. Clara is a registered HRD Corp trainer. WhatsApp us for the course code and claiming procedures.
What version of Microsoft Excel do I need?▼
Courses work with Excel 2019, 2021, and Microsoft 365. Some Advanced features (XLOOKUP, FILTER) require Excel 365 or 2021.
Do I need prior Excel knowledge for Beginner?▼
No prerequisites at all. The Beginner course starts from scratch — Ribbon, navigation, and data entry.
How long do I have access?▼
Member lifetime access. As long as your membership is active (RM 20/year renewal), you can revisit any enrolled course at any time. Renewal reminders are sent annually.
Is there a certificate?▼
Yes. A digital certificate of completion shareable on LinkedIn.
Can I enrol my whole team or company?▼
Yes. Group enrolments and customised in-house training available. WhatsApp +60 111 300 3886 for corporate proposals.
What payment methods are accepted?▼
Bank transfer (FPX), credit/debit card, and e-wallet. For corporate bookings and HRDC claims, contact us for invoicing.
